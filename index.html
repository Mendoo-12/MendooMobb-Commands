THE FLOW OF GOLD

The Royal Economy

Understand how MendoCoins move throughout Wisteria, where the money is stored, and how every citizen participates in the kingdom's economy.

THE FOUNDATION

One Kingdom. A Finite Supply.

MendoCoins are the official currency of Wisteria. Unlike a system that generates new coins whenever someone earns a reward, the Royal Economy is designed around a fixed supply.

This means money doesn't simply appear when someone works, gambles, or receives a payment. Instead, MendoCoins move between citizen wallets and the kingdom's financial institutions.

THE GOLDEN RULE Money leaving one account enters another.

A citizen can become richer, a casino can lose money, and the Treasury can grow or shrink. But these transactions do not change the total amount of MendoCoins in existence.

THE KINGDOM'S FUNDS

Where Are MendoCoins Stored?

All MendoCoins belong somewhere in the economy. Some are held by citizens, while others are stored in royal vaults that fund different activities.

Citizen Wallets

Every citizen has a personal MendoCoin balance. This is the money they can earn, spend, wager, invest, or use to repay debts.

The Four Royal Institutions

♛

Royal Treasury

Funds work payments, kingdom rewards, and other treasury-supported activities. It also receives designated fees and kingdom revenue.

⚄

Casino Vault

Holds the money backing casino games. Losing wagers add funds, while winning payouts draw from the vault.

▣

Royal Bank

Provides money for citizen loans. Loan payments leave the bank, while repayments return funds to the banking system.

⌁

Stock Exchange Reserve

Supports the stock market. Share purchases add funds to the reserve, while share sales are paid from its balance.

MONEY ENTERING CIRCULATION

How Do Citizens Receive MendoCoins?

Citizens can increase their personal balances through different kingdom activities. The important detail is that each payment has a source.

Working for the Kingdom

When a citizen completes a job, their earnings are paid from the Royal Treasury.

EXAMPLE: EARNING 3,000 MC Royal Treasury: -3,000 MC Citizen Wallet: +3,000 MC

Claiming Rewards

Daily claims and other treasury-funded rewards follow the same principle: existing money moves from the funding reserve to the citizen.

Winning at the Casino

When a citizen wins a casino game, the payout comes from the Casino Vault rather than being created from nothing.

Receiving a Loan

The Royal Bank can transfer money to a citizen through a loan. The citizen receives funds, but also takes on a debt that must be repaid.

Selling Investments

Citizens can receive money by selling their stock holdings. The Stock Exchange Reserve funds the sale according to the current market price and applicable fees.

MONEY RETURNING TO THE KINGDOM

Where Does Spent Money Go?

Earning is only half of the economy. For the kingdom to continue operating, MendoCoins must also flow back into its institutions.

Casino Losses

When a citizen loses a wager, the money goes to the Casino Vault.

EXAMPLE: LOSING A 5,000 MC WAGER Citizen Wallet: -5,000 MC Casino Vault: +5,000 MC

Stock Purchases

Purchasing shares transfers their purchase value into the Stock Exchange Reserve. Trading fees are collected by the Treasury.

EXAMPLE: BUYING 10,000 MC OF SHARES Citizen Wallet: -10,100 MC Stock Reserve: +10,000 MC Royal Treasury: +100 MC

Loan Repayments

When citizens repay their loans, the principal returns to the Royal Bank. Any applicable interest or fees follow the kingdom's lending rules.

The Royal Shop

The planned Royal Shop will give citizens another way to spend their wealth. Shop payments will return MendoCoins to designated kingdom reserves.

THE LIMITS OF WEALTH

What Happens When a Vault Runs Low?

The royal institutions do not possess unlimited money. Each operates using the funds available in its reserve.

For example, if the Casino Vault cannot cover a winning payout, the casino must refuse or restrict the transaction rather than create additional MendoCoins.

Likewise, the Royal Treasury cannot continue funding rewards indefinitely without money returning through the kingdom's economic activities.

This creates an important balance: earning and spending both matter.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

How Does Everything Stay Balanced?

Think of the Kingdom as one large economy divided into several accounts.

THE KINGDOM'S TOTAL MONEY SUPPLY All Citizen Wallets + Royal Treasury + Casino Vault + Royal Bank + Stock Exchange Reserve = Total MendoCoins

When one account gains money, another account loses the same amount. The distribution changes, but the total supply remains constant.

Stock shares and outstanding loan debts are tracked separately. They represent investments and obligations, not additional MendoCoins in circulation.

This is what makes Wisteria different: citizens aren't simply collecting points. They're participating in a shared economy where their decisions affect the movement of the kingdom's wealth.

THE CHRONICLES OF WISTERIA

Kingdom Lore

The history behind the crown, the coin, and the founding of the realm.

CHAPTER I

The Founding of the Kingdom

Before the kingdom's founding, MendoCoins flowed through the realm without a central treasury to account for every coin. A citizen's observation about money in circulation sparked an investigation of the kingdom's wealth.

The royal audit uncovered more than four trillion MendoCoins spread across 1,365 accounts. In response, Mad King Mendo proposed a one-time progressive founding tax to fund the Royal Treasury, Casino Vault, Royal Bank, and Stock Exchange Reserve.

Thus began the vision of Wisteria: a realm where wealth moves between citizens and institutions, instead of appearing from thin air.
